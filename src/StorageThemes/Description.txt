[h1]Storage Themes[/h1]

Adds painted theme sprites to storage bays. The appearance toggle opens a theme panel.

This is only a cosmetic mod. Storage capacity and contents are unchanged.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canisminor1990/probably-stolen-mods/main/src/StorageThemes/psd/screentshot.png[/img]

[h3]Languages[/h3]

UI text covers English and Chinese.

[h3]Features[/h3]

[list]
[*]Works on the storage bay and the large storage bay.
[*]Toggle appearance to open the theme panel, then pick a skin.
[*]Keeps the vanilla frames: Original, Orange, and Dark.
[*]Painted themes: Blank, Chemistry, Chip, Fish, Food, Grape, Household, Material, Medical, Plant, Luxury, Water, and Weapon.
[/list]

[h3]Settings[/h3]

[list]
[*]Open the Mods list, then click Settings on this mod.
[*]Theme art can be turned off. The game asks for a restart.
[/list]

[h1]储物舱涂装 / Storage Themes[/h1]

[i]给储物舱加上绘制的主题贴图。切换外观时打开涂装面板。[/i]

仅装饰，不改变容量和里面的东西。

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canisminor1990/probably-stolen-mods/main/src/StorageThemes/psd/screentshot.png[/img]

[h3]语言[/h3]

界面文本覆盖英语和中文。

[h3]功能[/h3]

[list]
[*]支持储物舱和大号储物舱。
[*]切换外观打开涂装面板，再点选一种涂装。
[*]保留游戏自带的三档：原图、橙色、深色。
[*]绘制的主题：空白、化学、芯片、鱼、食物、葡萄、日用、材料、医疗、植物、奢侈品、水、武器。
[/list]

[h3]设置[/h3]

[list]
[*]打开模组列表，点击本模组的「设置」。
[*]可以关闭主题涂装。游戏会要求重启。
[/list]
